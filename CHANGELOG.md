# Changelog

## Unreleased

- In development, no release yet.
- FAQ answers list the glossary terms they use in a row "Terms:" below the
  answer; hover a term for its explanation, click to open it in the glossary.
- Notifications show right away again: there was a two-minute pause
  between two notifications, so many seemed to be missing. A notification
  that is still on screen now counts new ones as "+N more", and answers to
  your own questions stay in front.
- Thread header: the line with category, language and expiry wraps next
  to "Mark as solved" and "Withdraw" instead of being cut off. Your own
  questions no longer show the right-click hint for the player menu.
- Glossary tooltips in FAQ answers now show when you hover over a blue term.
- FAQ links on answers: the FAQ button is off once 3 entries are attached,
  and the chips above the answer field always fit into the window (long
  questions are shortened, the tooltip shows them in full).
- Notifications never pop up during combat any more; they follow right
  after the fight. Whether they also wait in instances stays a setting.
- "Test" button for notifications in the settings, and the notification
  can be moved in Edit Mode (with "Reset position" in the settings).
- Glossary: over 100 game terms and chat abbreviations (Aggro, DoT, BoP, LFG,
  SW, BRD …) with a short explanation, as the last entry in the FAQ topics.
  Terms inside FAQ answers are highlighted; hover for the explanation,
  click to open it in the glossary.
- The one-question-per-minute limit is visible: "Ask a question" and the
  ask button in the dialog count down the seconds until the next question.
- Questions and answers show who wrote them with level, race and class
  (e.g. "Level 12 Night Elf Warrior"), in the list, the thread and pinned
  answers. Answers from older addon versions show the name only.
- New FAQ layout: pick a topic on the left, the questions are listed as
  bars below each other and a click unfolds the answer. Search stays, the
  FAQ language is now a dropdown, and the Wowhead hint is a highlighted
  banner across the top of the page.
- Fixed answer texts that were sometimes cut off with "…" until the
  window was moved (FAQ, FAQ suggestions and answers in threads).
- Look closer to the game's own windows: Blizzard window frame with the
  logo as portrait, the game's buttons, close buttons and colours, and one
  font everywhere (also in input fields).
- Solved questions stand out: check mark, green status and a green tint in
  the list, a green "This question is solved" bar in the thread.
  "Hide solved" on the Questions page hides them.
- Your own questions are highlighted in blue in the question list.
- New logo: a question mark in front of the globe of Azeroth.
- First-run setup: on the first login a short wizard asks for your
  languages, whether you are new or want to help as a mentor, which
  questions should notify you and a few extras. `/aa setup` or the button
  in the settings starts it again.
- Right click on an answer now always opens a menu, also on your own
  answers, with "Pin answer" in it.
- New look: dark leather-and-gold window with a header bar, navigation on
  the left, coloured categories, answer cards and an FAQ with list and
  detail view.
- Loads on retail (Midnight) too, for testing until Forever launches.
- FAQ suggestions while you ask: as you type the summary of a question,
  up to three matching FAQ entries appear. Click one to read the answer
  right there; "That helped" closes the dialog without asking.
  `/aa faqstats` shows how often that happened.
- Better FAQ search: finds entries by word, also with other word forms
  ("Berufe" finds "Beruf") and without accents, best matches first. The
  FAQ picker for mentors lists entries matching the question first.
- Bigger FAQ: over 50 typical beginner questions (first steps, quests,
  class and talents, combat, travel, gold, professions, groups, chat,
  interface) with a topic filter. "Ask a question" from the FAQ picks the
  matching category for you.
- FAQ links in answers: attach up to 3 FAQ entries to an answer with the
  book button; readers open them with one click, in their own FAQ language.
  Pinned answers keep their links. The FAQ has a topic per class and a topic
  dropdown.

- Main window (`/aa`) with the pages Questions, My questions, Pinned,
  Players, FAQ and Settings.
- Settings: mentor mode, minimap button, languages (one hidden channel each),
  language of new questions and how long your own questions are kept.
- Minimap button and addon compartment entry: left click opens the window,
  right click the settings.
- Questions and answers are checked and kept in memory: length and format
  checks, limits against spam, and questions disappear on their own (open
  after 2 hours, answered 45 minutes after the last answer, solved after
  10 minutes).
- Questions, answers, "solved" and withdrawals are exchanged with other
  players over the hidden language channels.
- Questions page: search, category filter, list of questions in your
  languages and the thread with all answers and when it disappears. Ask a
  question (category, summary, details, language), answer, mark your own
  question as solved or withdraw it.
- Your own player list (right-click a name): favourites (their questions
  first, marked with a star), ignored players (folded away), a note, "helped
  me" on answers to your questions, whisper. Players page with favourites,
  ignored and recently seen players. Kept on your computer only.
- Notifications: in mentor mode new questions per category (off, badge,
  sound), optionally only from certain classes or up to a level; questions
  from favourites and answers to your own questions always. A toast at most
  every 2 minutes ("+N more"), none in combat or instances, and a counter on
  the minimap button.
- The FAQ can now be shown in every language of the addon; translations
  follow topic by topic, until then entries appear in English. The FAQ points
  to Wowhead as the best place to look up quests, items and NPCs, with the
  address ready to copy, and has a new entry about it.
